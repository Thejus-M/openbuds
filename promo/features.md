# Features shown in the promo

## home.buds — Omarchy bar widget for Galaxy Buds2

1. Bar icon changes with noise mode (off, ANC, ambient, disconnected) and shows the lower earbud battery.
2. Battery per earbud and for the case: a ring for each bud, a capsule for the case. Last-known level stays, dimmed, when disconnected.
3. Earbud mark dims the side that sits in the case.
4. Noise control: a three-stop track — Off, ANC, Ambient. One click.
5. Ambient level: Low, Medium, High ladder, plus extra-high ambient.
6. Equalizer: six presets (Normal, Bass boost, Soft, Dynamic, Clear, Treble boost), each tile draws its curve.
7. Touch and hold per earbud: noise control, volume, voice assistant, Spotify, other.
8. Long-press cycle: pick which modes the long press goes through.
9. Switches: touchpad lock, game mode, noise control with one earbud, sidetone, call audio, spatial audio (firmware-gated).
10. Find My Earbuds: ring the buds from the panel, stop with one click.
11. Follow media: buds move between this PC and a KDE Connect phone.
12. Phone via PC: the phone plays into the PC, so every phone app reaches the buds.
13. Errors show in a banner; keyboard navigation through the whole panel.

## openbuds — the Linux version (any desktop)

14. Works with any BlueZ earbuds: connection, device list, connect, disconnect, battery.
15. Galaxy Buds2 extras: left, right and case battery, noise control, Phone via PC.
16. `openbuds` CLI: `status`, `status --json`, `waybar`, `devices`, `anc`.
17. `openbudsd` daemon holds the single SPP link and runs the Phone via PC rules.
18. `openbuds-tray`: tray icon with the lowest battery as a number; menu for Phone via PC and connection.
19. Phone battery and charging through KDE Connect.
